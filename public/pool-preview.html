From accepted work to a payout

Proposed payout design

Shared rewards · PPLNS

Payment depends on confirmed pool rewards and your accepted work in the relevant reward window.

1
Record accepted sharesStore each share’s worker, mode, difficulty, and timestamp durably.
2
Confirm the actual rewardReconcile rewards received by the pool on each chain before crediting miners.
3
Allocate the rewardUse the PPLNS window and difficulty-weighted work, with KAS and ZKAS accounted for separately.
4
Track the payoutReserve balances, persist the transaction, and reconcile confirmation before marking a payment complete.

How is SOLO different?

In SOLO mode, a confirmed reward is credited to the miner whose work found that block, after any published pool fee. SOLO work is accounted for separately from the shared reward window.

Would Stream Pool hold rewards before payout?

The proposed shared-payout service would receive rewards into pool-controlled wallets and then pay each miner. That makes it custodial between receipt and payout. Operating terms must explain this clearly before mining opens.

Would it use my existing community bridge settings?

The draft uses the existing ZKAS-first address layout as a starting point. The new pool’s software must confirm the exact endpoint, port, login format, and payout behavior before those settings become usable.

What about BTC, USDC, or USDT payouts?

The first version is planned around native KAS and ZKAS payouts. Conversion requires a separate exchange integration, rate handling, settlement, fees, and accounting.
