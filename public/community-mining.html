Both connections are solo merge-mining gateways. The 1.0.7 connection is being monitored separately while its per-miner KAS password routing is verified. Hashrates are bridge estimates; full wallets and IP addresses remain private.

Solo mining — rewards are not pooled or shared. Each miner competes independently. When a miner finds a ZKAS or KAS block, that reward follows that miner's configured payout routing. Blocks found by other miners are not distributed across the gateway.
