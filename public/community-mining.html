Mining at your own risk

ZKAS.stream is not responsible for miner operation, hardware failures, connection interruptions, or mining and payout results. Configure a backup pool in your miner in case this bridge connection drops. Mining and payouts after a switch to that pool follow its rules.

KAS payout display assumes the miner password contains a valid full kaspa: address. Bridge v1.0.12 no longer reports payout confirmation.
Solo mining — rewards are not pooled or shared. Each miner competes independently. When a miner finds a ZKAS or KAS block, that reward follows that miner's configured payout routing. Blocks found by other miners are not distributed across the gateway.
